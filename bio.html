About
Thibaut

Six years,
four projects

Thibaut is a lofi and ambient producer crafting introspective soundscapes rooted in a lifelong love of music. Raised in a musical household, his productions blend warmth and melancholy into music that feels like a quiet moment in a loud world.

BMI registered songwriter with placements on Spotify editorial playlists including lofi beats for insomniacs and minecore vol.1. Releasing on Spotify as NKN, sound of nunki, enkayen, and Dancing Room Only.

Based in Tomball, Texas. Home at LoFi Cafe Records.
