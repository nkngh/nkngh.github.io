About

Crafting worlds
through sound

NKN is an independent composer and sound designer specializing in lofi, ambient, and atmospheric soundscapes — music engineered for deep focus, creative flow, and quiet moments of reflection.

Drawing inspiration from the liminal spaces between waking and sleep, NKN's compositions weave organic textures with subtle electronic elements to create sound environments that feel both intimate and expansive.

"Music that exists in the space between thought and feeling."
