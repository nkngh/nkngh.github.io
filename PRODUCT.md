# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Music supervisors and sync licensors evaluating NKN for film, TV, ad, and game placements. Professionals who arrive via a pitch email or playlist, need to assess fit quickly, and want clear contact and licensing paths. Secondary: fans and collaborators who found the music organically.

## Product Purpose

Artist portfolio and electronic press kit (EPK) for NKN, an independent ambient and lofi composer. The site exists to convert professional interest into licensing conversations and bookings. Success looks like a music supervisor leaving with the audio they need, a press quote, and a way to reach out.

## Brand Personality

Serene, Intimate, Crafted. The music exists in the quiet space between thought and feeling; the site should feel the same way. Voice is warm but understated, never promotional. The work speaks; the site creates space for it.

## Anti-references

- Generic Spotify-clone UI: dark purple gradients, neon accents, streaming-service aesthetic
- Corporate music licensing sites: stock-photo heavy, overly formal, suit-and-tie energy
- Loud EDM / festival artist pages: flashy animations, intense colors, hype-first design

## Design Principles

1. **Let the music lead.** The interface exists to surface the work, not to decorate it. Every element earns its place by getting out of the way.
2. **Professional restraint.** Music supervisors are evaluating commercial fit; the design must signal credibility without trying too hard. Quiet confidence, not loud ambition.
3. **Crafted, not produced.** Organic quality comes from considered typography, texture, and spacing, not from templates or effects. Every detail is intentional.
4. **Warmth through material.** The warm, earthy palette and grain texture evoke analog recording culture: vinyl, cassette, studio. This is the aesthetic, not decoration.
5. **Trust through clarity.** Contact and licensing paths must be immediately legible. Professionals should never hunt for the thing they came for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Sufficient contrast on all text. Reduced-motion respect via `prefers-reduced-motion`. No flashing effects.
